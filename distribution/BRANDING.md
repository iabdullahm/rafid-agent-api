## Usage notes

- Coverage is **Muscat governorate**, not all of Oman — don't describe Rafid as nationwide or countrywide coverage anywhere it's branded. "Oman-specific" and "Oman property intelligence" are accurate because the product's domain and currency (OMR) are Oman-specific even though geographic coverage inside Oman is currently Muscat-only; don't let that shorthand drift into a nationwide-coverage claim.
- "Al Mouj" (or "Al Mouj Muscat") is accurate to name specifically because it's the area with real partner-fed sale data today — lead with it in any one-line pitch that needs a concrete example, rather than a generic "Oman-wide" claim.
- Rafid is agent-first, not a human dashboard product — branding copy should keep "Discover. Pay per call. Execute." or an equivalent agent-facing framing as the primary message, with any human-readable landing page positioned as secondary/compatibility framing, matching how `PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION` in `src/api/manifest.ts` itself is written ("Not designed primarily as a human dashboard product").
- Don't invent a marketplace-specific badge, certification, or "verified" claim — see [`A2A.md`](A2A.md)'s closing note and [`MARKETPLACE-LISTING.md`](MARKETPLACE-LISTING.md).
